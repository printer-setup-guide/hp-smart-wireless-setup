OfficeJet Setup FAQ

How do I connect my OfficeJet Pro to a 5GHz network?

Access the wireless settings from the printer's touchscreen. Ensure your router is broadcasting a 5GHz band, select it from the list, and enter your WPA2 password. Note that some older OfficeJet models only support 2.4GHz.

Why is the Automatic Document Feeder (ADF) jamming?

Lift the ADF cover and check for staples, paper clips, or torn paper blocking the rollers. Clean the separation pad with distilled water and ensure you are not loading more than 35 sheets at a time.
