HP Setup Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my HP printer to a new Wi-Fi network?

Press and hold the Wireless and Cancel buttons together for 3 seconds to reset network settings. Then run HP Smart setup on your device to connect to your new Wi-Fi network.

What should I do if my HP printer says Offline?

Check that both your computer and HP printer are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network. Restart your router, printer, and PC, and set HP Printer as default in Settings.

How do I clear a false Paper Jam error?

Turn off the printer and unplug it from the wall. Check the input tray, output tray, and rear access door for tiny scraps of paper. Clean the paper feed rollers with a lightly dampened lint-free cloth to restore grip, then plug the printer back in.

Why is my printer rejecting my new Ink Cartridges?

Ensure you have removed the protective plastic tape from the cartridge contacts. If the error persists, gently clean the copper-colored contacts on the cartridge and inside the carriage with a dry cotton swab, then reseat them firmly.

How do I manage my HP Ink Subscription?

You can check your page count, update your payment methods, or modify your subscription tier directly through the HP Smart App dashboard by clicking on your account profile and selecting the subscription management tab.

How do I find my HP Printer IP address?

Print a Network Configuration Page from the printer settings menu or control panel to view the IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.XX).
