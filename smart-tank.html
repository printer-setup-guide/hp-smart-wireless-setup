First-Time Smart Tank Installation

Unlike standard printers, the HP Smart Tank series requires manual ink filling and printhead priming before it can be connected to Wi-Fi. Do not connect the USB cable or run the software until the physical ink setup and alignment page printing are complete.

Smart Tank Setup FAQ

How do I correctly install the Smart Tank printheads?

Wait for the carriage to move to the center. Remove the orange plastic plugs from both printheads, taking care not to touch the copper contacts. Press them firmly into their color-coded slots until they click, then close the blue latch.

Why are there white lines in my color prints?

This indicates a clogged printhead or air in the ink tubes. Run the 'Clean Printhead' diagnostic tool from the HP Smart App up to three times to flush ink through the system.
