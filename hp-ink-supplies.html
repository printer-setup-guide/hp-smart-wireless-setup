HP Ink FAQ

Why does my printer say "Cartridge Cannot Be Used"?

This occurs if you are using an HP Ink Subscription cartridge but have cancelled your account, or if the printer has lost internet access for too long. Subscription cartridges immediately deactivate upon billing cancellation.

How do I cancel my HP Ink Subscription?

Log into the HP Smart App, select your printer, click on the HP Instant Ink dashboard, go to 'Account Details', select 'My Plan', and choose 'Cancel my HP Instant Ink Subscription'. You must purchase retail ink to continue printing.
